# SS Wayfarer — v0.3 split-level atrium draft

## Identity and layout
SS Wayfarer is the copper/bronze mothership. Firefly WF-01 is its separate Pathfinder-class mission cruiser, with one cabin, equipment lockers and compact quarantine alcove. The user’s hand sketch supersedes the old isolated room shapes. No Xenobiology room upstairs and no Captain's Quarters downstairs.

- Upper: tapered Command Bridge in bow; Holodeck and Captain's Quarters port; Crew Lounge and Media Nexus starboard; split-level atrium hall; Library and physical Halcyon brain in electrogel aft.
- Lower: Firefly bow launch hangar; Medbay and Xenobiology port; Mess Hall/Galley and Maintenance/Storage starboard (the supporting maintenance area remains a draft); Engine Room / Halcyon power core directly below Library.
- The aft perimeter becomes a continuous transition walkway and twin mirrored stairs, rather than empty gaps or isolated spiral towers. External engine nacelles are not habitable rooms.
- Only the lower bow rotates down, on the same pivot as the interior triangular dock. Bridge remains stationary. Firefly deploys out of the lower bow.

## Geometry
Original source GLB is normalized, forward −Z, +Y up; the existing prototype scale of 120 is retained for comparison, not a verified physical size. All new room and assembled deck models use shared coordinates; Firefly exterior and cabin are local-space exceptions. Upper floor +0.65, lower floor −5.3 (5.95 separation); both room heights 3.2. Core axis x=0,z=36; upper radius 10.2, lower radius 9.4. The central hall occupies z −24 to 25.8; maximum width 9.2 units, tapered to 5 at both ends. Upper floor contains a 4.1 × 35.6 capsule-shaped opening (~142.34 square units), with nominal 2.35-unit side walkways, guardrails and fore/aft crossovers. Lower hall has a continuous floor and no ceiling blocking the opening. Room doors move to x ±4.6; outer hull-derived profiles and deck heights are retained. Only the existing twin aft stairs connect decks; no new central stairs are assumed. The upper interior has a transparent skylight panel; the original exterior glass material/roof opening remains unverified. See layout.json for piecewise hull-derived width profiles and bevelled room polygons. Upper core and lower reactor use the same axis. Stairs follow side/aft arcs of radius 11.35, width 1.8 and 34 treads (rise about 0.175 per tread); upper aft apron has openings for the stair sweep. Original lower jaw hinge remains at (0, −7.1, −32), with provisional 37° opening. Dock top adjusted to revised lower floor. Firefly is about 10 prototype units long.

## Authority and limitations
The room widths were derived from sampled vertical intersections of the actual uploaded mobile hull at the retained scale, with conservative side margins and a separate upper/lower envelope. The envelope is not a measurement of all usable interior volume; source hull is irregular and not a watertight engineered pressure shell. The top-view silhouette includes volumes too shallow to accommodate standing rooms, particularly the tapered nose and outer nacelles. The draft room footprints now partition almost all of the selected plan envelope, including aft walkways. This does not prove full 3D fit or certify the entire exterior volume as habitable. The original hull remains intact except for the previously established planar jaw split. Actual mesh top-face projection confirms an open and connected upper loop, full lower floor, and 36 sampled corridor route edges pass 0.25-unit planar clearance. Boundary sampling passes for ten polygonal rooms and Library, but 6 of 32 engine-room perimeter samples remain outside the current vertical envelope. The aft apron, stair headroom, roof clearances, props, collisions, launch sweep and cabin fit are not certified. Lower aft hull remodeling may be necessary. No live gameplay integration or photoreal materials claimed. Do not overwrite live game until these issues are addressed.
